# Product: Help'Em

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS

## Users
Residents of the adopted partner barangay, Purok/Sitio leaders, vulnerable households (seniors, families with infants), barangay officials, and MMSU NSTP (National Service Training Program) student volunteers and community facilitators.

## Product Purpose
Help'Em is a hyper-local community emergency and live update platform created as an MMSU NSTP (National Service Training Program) civic engagement initiative, specifically designed to protect, support, and connect our adopted partner community during severe typhoons, floods, power outages, and civic emergencies.

## Positioning
Unlike impersonal national alert systems or chaotic social media feeds, Help'Em is a localized digital lifeline built with love and precision by MMSU NSTP students for their partner community—combining real-time barangay ground truth, vulnerable neighbor check-ins, student volunteer mobilization, and mutual aid with Apple-grade clarity and dignity.

## Operating Context
High-stress emergency situations (severe weather, utility outages, local hazards, mutual aid calls) on mobile and desktop web browsers, potentially under degraded connectivity or low power, as well as routine neighborhood preparedness check-ins.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Hyper-local incident feeds, live status verification, and urgent safety check-ins.
- AI-synthesized situational summaries distilled from community inputs and official dispatches.
- Community mutual aid board (supplies, power, shelter, transport, volunteer mobilization).
- Extreme legibility, fast rendering, and high-contrast visual hierarchy.
- Stack: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS.

## Brand Commitments
- Brand Name: Help'Em (friendly, decisive community emergency network).
- Unmatched visual craft, premium aesthetic, and obsessive attention to detail.
- Apple UI aestheticness: Inspired by Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Apple Emergency SOS / Watch Ultra precision—frosted glass vibrancy, continuous squircle curvature, pristine typography hierarchy, and calm, reassuring clarity.
- Pinned Color Palette: Interchanged light palette—crisp optic white ground (#FFFFFF and #F8FAF9), authoritative deep hunter/forest green typography, navbar, and primary CTA (#06391C / #074020), translucent frosted glass console with subtle hairline borders, and Apple system accents (emerald/mint #30D158, amber #FF9F0A, emergency red #FF453A, gold #FBBF24).
- Pinned Typography: Helvetica / Helvetica Neue and Swiss neo-grotesque hierarchy—tight optical kerning (tracking-tight), crisp horizontal terminals, authoritative weights (bold display, clean medium UI labels, elegant italic accents).
- Hero Star: The official MMSU mascot, rendered as a faithful full-body 3D character with zero distortion, wearing the green MMSU jersey with yellow lettering and matching shorts.
- Calm, authoritative, and empathetic tone—rejecting alarmist sensationalism.

## Evidence on Hand
- Demonstration datasets and synthetic incident streams to be authored at full fidelity with explicit synthetic labeling.

## Product Principles
- Clarity Over Chaos: Reduce cognitive load in high-stress moments through deliberate visual hierarchy.
- Ground-Truth Trust: Clearly distinguish verified emergency updates from crowd-sourced notes.
- Actionable Relief: Every critical notice provides a direct, unambiguous path to safety or aid.
- Calm Authority: Premium, meticulously crafted design inspires confidence, dignity, and solidarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text, WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance for vital alerts, accessible keyboard navigation, and screen reader-optimized update feeds.
